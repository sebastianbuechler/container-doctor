<Aside title="What is going on in it">
Paperless is down: its Postgres database keeps failing its health check after
hitting its memory limit, and a restart of it is **waiting for approval**.
Sonarr's indexer rejects its API key, `/srv/data` is full in about 17 days, and
one disk has remapped sectors. Earlier in the week the doctor restarted a hung
Jellyfin and a locked Uptime Kuma, and checked that both came back.
</Aside>

## What is real and what is not

Everything you see was produced by the doctor's own code: the rules opened the
incidents, the policy proposed the restart, the briefing was written from the
same facts, and the memory suggestions come from two weeks of samples. What
stood in for the outside world: Docker, the host's `/proc` and `/sys`, the
SMART exporter, the registries, the network checks - and the model, whose
answers are canned. The recipe is in the repository, under `demo/`.

Being a recording, it changes nothing: approving, dismissing, refreshing or
saving a setting answers with a note that this is a demo, and times shift so
the recording always looks recent.
